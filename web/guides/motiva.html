Motiva Flora Tissue Expander Directions for Use

Establishment Labs publishes the US directions for use for the Motiva Flora tissue expander on its eIFU portal. Unlike magnetic port expanders, it has MR Conditional labeling at 1.5 T and 3 T.

  • Category Breast Tissue Expanders
  • Covers Motiva Flora SmoothSilk tissue expander, which uses an RFID port locator instead of a magnet, and its port locator.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Confirm from the operative note or implant card that the patient has a Motiva Flora expander, not a magnetic port expander from another manufacturer.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Motiva eIFU portal ↗Choose the product type that starts with "US FDA cleared", not "Outside US". The portal needs JavaScript.ifu.motiva.health
  1. Open the Motiva eIFU portal, choose the US FDA cleared Flora tissue expander, and download the directions for use.
  2. Read the MRI section, including field strength, spatial gradient, SAR, and the patient conditions.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The document shows its number, revision, and date, "DOC-001016 Rev. 3, NOV2024", for the US directions for use.
  • US editions give the Motiva USA address in Santa Barbara, California.

! Watch For

  • The portal also lists an "Outside US" edition, DOC-037. Its conditions differ slightly, and it also contains a Federal law sentence, so that sentence alone does not identify the US edition.
  • "Motiva Flora Port Locator is MR Unsafe and should not be brought into an MR Scanner."
  • Its MR Conditional labeling does not extend to magnetic port expanders. Identify the manufacturer and model before using these conditions.

It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
