Boston Scientific ImageReady MRI Technical Guides

Boston Scientific publishes an MRI Technical Guide for each therapy type. The US ImageReady pages and Model Lookup lead to the guide for your generator and lead combination.

  • Category Cardiac Devices
  • Covers ImageReady MR Conditional transvenous pacing and defibrillation systems. The S-ICD and Boston Scientific neuromodulation systems have separate guides.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the generator model and every lead model from the device ID card or device clinic records.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

ImageReady MR-Conditional Systems (US) ↗Resources, checklists, and links to MRI technical guides.bostonscientific.comImageReady Model Lookup (US) ↗Lists generator and lead models with their 1.5 T and 3 T system configurations.bostonscientific.comProduct literature library ↗Searchable manual library. It needs JavaScript.bostonscientific.com
  1. Use the US Model Lookup to see which system configurations include your models.
  2. Find the MRI Technical Guide for the therapy type, such as pacing or defibrillation. The guides are separate.
  3. Read the guide's identity line on the back cover before using any of its content.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The back cover ends with a document number, language, market, and date, for example "51897537-001 EN US 2024-10".
  • The first page carries the "CAUTION - Federal law (USA)" statement for US labeling.
  • Every generator and lead model in the patient's system appears in the guide's configuration tables.

! Watch For

  • European editions are hosted on the same site. A search can return a guide whose back cover reads "EN Europe", such as "359259-037 EN Europe 2020-01".
  • A newer US edition can have a new document number. The US pacing guide moved from 360167-003 (2019-07) to 51897537-001 (2024-10), so searching only for the old number misses the update.
  • The product literature library can look empty to tools that do not run JavaScript.

Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Boston Scientific publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
