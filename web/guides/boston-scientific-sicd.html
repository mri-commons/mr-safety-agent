Boston Scientific ImageReady S-ICD MRI Technical Guide

Boston Scientific publishes one MRI Technical Guide for its subcutaneous ICD systems. The US edition covers 1.5T scanning of the complete system, with the pulse generator in MRI Protection Mode.

  • Category Cardiac Devices
  • Covers EMBLEM S-ICD (A209) and EMBLEM MRI S-ICD (A219) pulse generators with Boston Scientific or Cameron Health subcutaneous electrodes. Transvenous pacing and defibrillation systems have a separate guide.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the pulse generator model and the electrode model from the patient ID card or device clinic records, and ask whether any other leads, adaptors, or abandoned devices are implanted.

  1. Open the US product literature library and search for "EMBLEM". Open the MRI Technical Guide listed as MRI Information.
  2. Confirm the generator and electrode appear in the guide's table of ImageReady S-ICD System components. For an A209, confirm the firmware upgrade the table requires.
  3. Read the cardiology and radiology conditions, and the MRI Protection Mode section, including its time-out.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The back cover shows the document number, language, market, and date, "92346926-001 EN US 2019-07", for the US guide.
  • The cover carries "CAUTION: Federal law (USA) restricts this device to sale by or on the order of a physician".
  • The guide's component table lists "All models" of Boston Scientific and Cameron Health S-ICD electrodes.

! Watch For

  • The edition found most easily by web search reads "92346927-001 EN Europe 2020-09" on its back cover. Its conditions match, but it lists specific electrodes and is not US labeling.
  • Boston Scientific states: "Only A209 models that have been upgraded (firmware version 3.1.529 or later upgrade to add MRI protections mode) are MRI compatible."
  • The older Cameron Health SQ-RX Model 1010 pulse generator is not in the guide's component table. Electrode manuals that call an electrode "compatible" with it are describing the connection, not MRI status.
  • The ImageReady page's manual link can redirect to another country's library. Choose the United States before searching.

Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Boston Scientific publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
