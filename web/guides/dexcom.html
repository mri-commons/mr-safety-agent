GUIDE

Dexcom CGM User Guides

Dexcom puts the MRI statement in the Contraindications section near the front of each CGM user guide. Its US guides pages link the current PDF for each system.

  • Category Diabetes Devices
  • Covers Dexcom G7, G7 15 Day, G6, and G6 Pro, and the Stelo over-the-counter biosensor.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Find out which Dexcom system the patient wears, such as G7, G7 15 Day, G6, or Stelo. The app, receiver, or sensor packaging shows the name.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Dexcom user guides (US) ↗Current user guides for G7, G7 15 Day, G6, and G6 Pro, in English and US Spanish.dexcom.comStelo user guides ↗Current guide for the over-the-counter Stelo biosensor.stelo.com
  1. Open the US guides page, or the Stelo guides page, and download the user guide for that exact system.
  2. Read the Contraindications section near the front of the guide. It covers MRI, CT, and diathermy in one statement.
  3. Check the symbols table near the end of the guide for the MR Unsafe symbol.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The back cover ends with the document number, revision, and date, for example "AW00078-10 Rev 005 MT-00078-10 Rev Date: 05/2026" for the US G7 guide.
  • US prescription guides state that "U.S. law restricts the sale of" the system. US English document numbers end in -10.
  • US guides use mg/dL and list no EC REP or UK REP.

! Watch For

  • The San Diego address is printed on UK and Canadian editions too. A UK G7 guide, "AW00046-05 Rev 006", lists European and UK representatives and uses mmol/L. Use the US edition.
  • CT rules differ between generations. G7, G7 15 Day, and Stelo allow a CT scan with the sensor kept out of the scanned area and covered by a lead apron. The G6 guides say not to wear the CGM for CT at all. Every heading reads "No MRI/CT/diathermy", so read the full statement.
  • Safety summary pages can link older editions. Stelo's safety page linked Rev 002 of the user guide (03/2025), while its guides page linked Rev 004 (07/2026). Start from the guides pages.
Step 4 of 4

Check Notices and Related Pages

Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Dexcom publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
