Sophysa Polaris Instructions for Use

Sophysa USA posts the Polaris valve instructions for use, and the instructions for its adjustment and reading tools, as PDFs on its US product page. The MRI section is in the valve IFU.

  • Category Shunt Valves
  • Covers Polaris adjustable valves (SPV, SPVA, and SPVB, including SiphonX versions) and their adjustment and reading tools. Older Sophy adjustable valves are not sold in the US.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the valve model and implant date from the operative record or the patient's valve card, such as SPVA or SPV-SX, and the last recorded pressure setting.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Polaris product page (US) ↗Links the current US instructions for the valve, the PAK2 adjustment kit, and the PAK3-ERI reading instrument.sophysa.us
  1. Open the US Polaris product page and download the valve instructions for use.
  2. Read section 7 on MRI, and the section on reading the operating pressure after implantation.
  3. If the setting will be read or adjusted around the scan, open the instructions for the PAK2 kit or PAK3-ERI instrument as well. Both tools are MR Unsafe.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The back cover shows the document code, revision, and date, "NT118_EN_US Rev000 - 2023-11", for the US valve IFU.
  • US editions state "Federal (USA) Law restricts this device to sale by or on the order of a physician." and point to www.sophysa.us without a CE mark.
  • Text extracted from the US IFU cover also contains an older code, "NT118 (EN-FR) Rev007", which does not show on the page. Use the identity on the back cover.

! Watch For

  • The bilingual English-French edition "NT118 (EN-FR) Rev007 - 2015-07" also has the Federal law sentence and a US address, but shows the CE mark. Its MRI wording matches, yet it is not the current US edition.
  • Sophysa's US MRI Guide (DCGu-MG-01(USA) Rev000) lists a 4 W/kg First Level Controlled Mode allowance that the IFU does not give. The IFU is the labeling. Report the difference.
  • Sophysa states: "it is recommended that the pressure setting of the valve is checked before and after the MRI examination or following exposure to a powerful magnetic field."
  • The fixed-pressure Sophy Mini SM1 has its own, newer MRI table. Do not apply it to Polaris.
Step 4 of 4

Check Notices and Related Pages

FDA Medical Device Recalls and Early Alerts ↗Sophysa has no US notice page. Check FDA's lists.fda.gov

Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
