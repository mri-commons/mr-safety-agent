Codman Programmable Valve Instructions for Use

Integra publishes the instructions for Codman valves on its eIFU portal, filtered by country. CERTAS Plus has two catalog-number families, and each has its own instructions with different MRI conditions.

  • Category Shunt Valves
  • Covers CODMAN CERTAS Plus and CODMAN HAKIM programmable valves, including the HAKIM lumboperitoneal valve, and their tool kits.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the valve model and catalog number from the operative record or the patient's valve card, such as 828801PL for a CERTAS Plus valve, and the last recorded setting.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Integra eIFU portal ↗Choose United States as the country, then search by product. Each document's page says whether it is the most recent or superseded.labeling.integralife.com
  1. Open the Integra eIFU portal, choose United States, and search for the valve name.
  2. Open the IFU the portal lists for the patient's catalog number. Confirm the portal marks it as the most recent edition.
  3. Read the MRI section and the instructions for verifying the valve setting after MRI.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The back page shows the document number, revision, and date, for example "209026001 Rev B 2024-05" for the US CERTAS Plus IFU.
  • On the portal, the US CERTAS Plus IFU is titled "USA" and is listed for catalog numbers ending in PL.
  • Each document page on the portal shows "Most recent" or names the edition that supersedes it.

! Watch For

  • CERTAS Plus valves without the PL suffix have other instructions, 206521-001 and 208878-001, with a lower spatial gradient limit. The outside-US twin of the US IFU, 209027001 Rev A OUS, also gives 1,000 Gauss/cm (10 T/m) instead of 20 T/m. Match the catalog number to the IFU.
  • HAKIM and CERTAS Plus behave differently in MRI. The HAKIM IFU states that MR systems up to 3T "may change the setting of the valve." The CERTAS Plus IFU states the mechanism "is resistant to unintended changes in the setting in a 3 Tesla MRI." Both require confirming the setting after MRI.
  • The HAKIM lumboperitoneal valve has its own IFU with different MRI conditions from the HAKIM ventricular valves.
  • The tool kits and programmers are MR Unsafe and must stay out of the MR suite.
Step 4 of 4

Check Notices and Related Pages

FDA recall database ↗Integra has no US notice page for these valves. Search FDA records by product name, such as CERTAS Plus.accessdata.fda.govFDA Medical Device Recalls and Early Alerts ↗FDA's list of recalls and early alerts.fda.gov
Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Integra publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
