MED-EL Hearing Implant MRI Documents

MED-EL publishes US MRI documents on its US Important Safety Information page. After you choose a system, the page links the US Medical Procedures manual, an MRI checklist, and an MRI safety status overview.

  • Category Hearing Implants
  • Covers MED-EL cochlear implants such as SYNCHRONY 2, SYNCHRONY, and MED-EL CONCERT, plus other MED-EL hearing implants
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the exact implant model, including any PIN variant, from the patient ID card. MED-EL also suggests contacting the implanting clinic if the model is unclear.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Important Safety Information (US) ↗Confirm the country is United States, then choose a system such as Cochlear Implant Systems. Document links appear only after you choose. The page needs JavaScript.medel.comMRI and cochlear implants (US) ↗Background page whose "Info for Radiologists" link leads to the US safety information page.medel.com
  1. Open the US Important Safety Information page, confirm United States, and choose the system type.
  2. Open the Medical Procedures manual listed for the US. Its MRI checklist and status overview are companion documents.
  3. In the manual, go to the section for the exact model and read its MRI subsection. Each model has its own section.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • US documents say "(English US)" next to the document number, for example "AW33290_9.0 (English US)".
  • The back page states the revision and issue date, for example "Document revision 9.0 (issued 2024-08)".
  • The companion documents have their own revisions and dates. Open each one from the US page rather than from a saved copy.

! Watch For

  • The short link medel.com/isi, printed in some documents, opens the international page, which serves European editions. Navigate to the US page instead.
  • Model names differ by region. European documents use names such as CONCERTO, while US documents use MED-EL CONCERT. Searching a European name can lead to a European document.
  • SYNCHRONY, SYNCHRONY 2, and PIN variants have separate sections. Do not read across them.
  • Older revisions linger in search results, and some of their links no longer work. Start from the US page.
Step 4 of 4

Check Notices and Related Pages

FDA Medical Device Recalls and Early Alerts ↗MED-EL does not post a separate US safety-notice page. Check FDA's lists.fda.gov
Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where MED-EL publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
