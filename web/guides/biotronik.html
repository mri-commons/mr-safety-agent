BIOTRONIK ProMRI Technical Manuals

BIOTRONIK puts its US MRI labeling for cardiac systems in one document, the ProMRI System Technical Manual. Its Manual Library shows the current US revision and every earlier one.

  • Category Cardiac Devices
  • Covers BIOTRONIK ProMRI pacemakers, CRT-Ps, ICDs, CRT-Ds, and leads in the US. Insertable cardiac monitors have MRI details in their own manuals.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the generator model and every lead and port plug, with order numbers, from the patient ID card or implant records.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

BIOTRONIK Manual Library ↗Choose United States (USA), then the product "ProMRI System". The page shows each revision and its publication date. It needs JavaScript.manuals.biotronik.comProMRI (US) ↗Overview with links to the Manual Library and ProMRI SystemCheck.biotronik.comProMRI SystemCheck ↗Checks whether an implanted BIOTRONIK system is MR Conditional in a chosen country. It needs JavaScript.promricheck.com
  1. In the Manual Library, choose United States (USA) and open "ProMRI System". US entries end in _US. Download the newest revision.
  2. Find the generator and every lead in the manual's tables, and read the footnotes on lead position. All parts must appear together as one listed system.
  3. Also open the generator's own US technical manual. It refers to the ProMRI manual for MR conditions of use.
  4. Cross-check the system in ProMRI SystemCheck with the United States selected.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The back cover shows the document number with its revision letter and date, for example "M4197-V 06/26".
  • US editions carry "Federal (U.S.A.) law restricts this device to sale by, or on the order of, a physician" and name BIOTRONIK, Inc. as distributor.
  • The revision letter in the PDF should match the revision the library marks as current for the United States.

! Watch For

  • International editions look similar. A search can return a "ProMRI MR Conditional device systems Technical Manual" with a CE mark and a different document number. It is not US labeling, and its system combinations differ.
  • BIOTRONIK states: "approval is only given for the specific combinations of lead(s) and devices" and "the approval status may vary between countries."
  • Older US revisions and an older US checklist still open from direct links. Reach the manual through the library with the United States selected.

Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where BIOTRONIK publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
