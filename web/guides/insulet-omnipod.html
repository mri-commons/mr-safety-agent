Omnipod User Guides

Insulet publishes several US guides for each Omnipod system, and each one includes the MRI statement. Its US product guides page links the current editions.

  • Category Diabetes Devices
  • Covers Omnipod 5, with the Insulet Controller, Android, or iPhone, and Omnipod DASH tubeless insulin pumps.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Find out which Omnipod system the patient uses, whether it runs on the Insulet Controller or a phone, and which CGM, if any, is paired with it.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Omnipod product guides (US) ↗Current US guides for Omnipod 5 and Omnipod DASH, in English and Spanish.omnipod.com
  1. Open the US product guides page and download the Technical User Guide for that system and controller. It has the fullest MRI wording.
  2. Read the Contraindications and the "X-rays, MRIs, and CT Scans" section.
  3. For a paired CGM, open the CGM's own US user guide. The current Omnipod 5 guides defer to the sensor's instructions.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The cover and back cover show the part number, revision, and date, for example "PT-002951-AW Rev. 03 2026-04-06" for the US Omnipod 5 Technical User Guide.
  • US editions state "Caution: Federal (US) law restricts this device to sale by or on the order of a physician."
  • US editions list the US Controller model, PDM-H001-G-XX for Omnipod 5 or PDM-USA1-D001-MG-USA1 for DASH.

! Watch For

  • The removal requirement changed between US editions with the same Controller model. The 2022 Omnipod 5 guide (PT-000369-AW Rev. 009 08/22) lists the Pod, CGM transmitter, and CGM sensor. The 2026 edition lists the Pod and defers to the sensor's instructions. Check the part number and revision.
  • UK and Canadian guides use a different Controller model, PDM-M001-G-MM, and list the CGM sensors for removal. The omnipod.com/safety page also still uses the older wording. Use the current US guide.
  • PDF file names do not always match the guide titles. Check the title and part number on the cover.
