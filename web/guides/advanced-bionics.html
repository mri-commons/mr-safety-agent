Advanced Bionics MRI Safety Information

Advanced Bionics publishes MRI safety information on a page for each country, and the full device instructions for use in a separate US library.

  • Category Hearing Implants
  • Covers HiRes Ultra 3D, HiRes Ultra, and earlier HiRes 90K and Clarion cochlear implants
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the exact implant model and electrode from the patient card or operative record, such as CI-1601 for HiRes Ultra 3D or CI-1600 for HiRes Ultra.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

MRI Safety Information (United States) ↗The page heading should read United States. It links a HiRes Ultra 3D MRI safety document and per-implant information that loads with JavaScript.advancedbionics.comInstructions for Use library (United States) ↗US device instructions, filterable by product and sortable by revision date. It needs JavaScript.advancedbionics.com
  1. Open the US MRI Safety Information page directly. The general advancedbionics.com/mri address opens a country picker instead.
  2. Open the MRI information for the exact implant. For HiRes Ultra 3D, download the numbered MRI safety document under Downloads.
  3. Cross-check with the implant's US instructions for use, filtered by product and sorted newest first.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The numbered US MRI document ends with its number, revision, and date, for example "029-Q721-03 Rev A 2021-10-20".
  • The US instructions for use carry a Federal law caution, and their library is marked for use within the United States.
  • Some per-implant pages are image-only captures without a document number. Compare them with the numbered document and the instructions for use.

! Watch For

  • Advanced Bionics states: "Conditions vary by geography." The country picker lists many regional pages with similar file names. Use only the United States page.
  • HiRes Ultra and HiRes Ultra 3D have different MRI pathways, and electrode variants have separate instructions. Use the documents for the exact model.
  • An older edition can differ from the current one by a single character in its document number, with the same revision letter. Check the full number and date against the US library.
Step 4 of 4

Check Notices and Related Pages

FDA Medical Device Recalls and Early Alerts ↗Advanced Bionics does not keep a separate US safety-notice page. Check FDA's lists.fda.gov
Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Advanced Bionics publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
