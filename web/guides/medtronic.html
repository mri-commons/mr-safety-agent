Medtronic MRI Technical Manuals

Medtronic publishes an MRI technical manual for each MRI SureScan system. Its US MRI Resource Library finds the manual by device model number.

  • Category Cardiac Devices
  • Covers MRI SureScan pacemakers, defibrillators, and leads (cardiac rhythm devices), including the Aurora EV-ICD extravascular defibrillator. Medtronic neuromodulation systems and shunt valves have separate guides.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the model number of the device and of every lead from the patient's device ID card or the device clinic's records.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

MRI Resource Library (US) ↗Search by model number. Confirm that the United States region is selected.manuals.medtronic.comMRI Resources for healthcare professionals (US) ↗Overview pages, the MR Conditional Search Tool, and patient resources.medtronic.com
  1. Open the MRI Resource Library, confirm the US region, and search for the device model number.
  2. Open the MRI technical manual the library lists for that model. It covers the whole SureScan system, not only the device.
  3. Confirm each lead. Medtronic requires a complete SureScan system, and some leads are MR Conditional only in specific versions or lengths.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The back cover shows the manual's document number and date, for example "M964377A001 B, 2016-10-19".
  • The cover carries the "Caution - Federal law (USA)" statement, which marks US labeling.
  • Your device model appears in the manual's model list.
  • A manual can be several years old and still current. The library's listing for your model, not the date alone, shows which manual applies.

! Watch For

  • The MR-Conditional Cardiac Device Summary Chart and similar overviews are dated secondary documents. Use the technical manual for conditions.
  • The MR Conditional Search Tool needs JavaScript and may look empty in some browsers or tools.
  • Medtronic states: "Do not use magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) on patients who have non-MR conditional versions/lengths of these leads implanted as part of a complete SureScan System."
  • The automatic SureScan time-out differs by device. The Azure pacemaker manual states that SureScan mode turns off 24 hours after it is turned on, while the Aurora EV-ICD manual (M000022C001 C) states 6 hours. Check the manual for the patient's device.

Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Medtronic publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
