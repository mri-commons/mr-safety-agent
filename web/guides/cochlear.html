Cochlear Nucleus MRI Guidelines

Cochlear publishes one MRI guidelines document for health professionals in each region. The US edition covers all current and older Nucleus implant models.

  • Category Hearing Implants
  • Covers Cochlear Nucleus cochlear implants, including the CI1000, CI600, CI500, and CI24 series
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the exact implant model from the patient's implant card or clinic records. The guidelines also describe how to identify a model.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

MRI guidelines for professionals (US) ↗Links the "Nucleus System MRI Guidelines (US)" document.cochlear.com
  1. Open the US professionals page and download the Nucleus System MRI Guidelines (US).
  2. Find the section and tables for the exact model. Magnet, kit, and SAR conditions can differ between models in the same series.
  3. Check whether the patient has implants on both sides or other implants. The guidelines explain how to handle bilateral recipients.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The cover reads "United States of America" and "For Professionals".
  • The back cover shows the document number and date, for example "D2044134-V2 2025-05".

! Watch For

  • Cochlear publishes separate editions for other regions, such as Canada and Asia Pacific. Make sure the cover says United States of America.
  • Older editions and third-party copies remain online. Use the document linked from the US professionals page.
  • Read the row for the exact model. Limits that apply to one model in a series may not apply to another.
Step 4 of 4

Check Notices and Related Pages

FDA - Cochlear Implants and MRI Safety ↗General FDA information for patients and providers.fda.gov
Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Cochlear publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
