MiniMed Pump and Sensor User Guides

MiniMed publishes a system user guide for each pump and sensor combination, plus a separate guide for each sensor and transmitter. Its US download library has one page per product.

  • Category Diabetes Devices
  • Covers MiniMed 780G, 770G, 630G, and Flex insulin pumps, and Simplera, Simplera Sync, Guardian 4, and Instinct sensors. MiniMed was formerly Medtronic Diabetes.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the pump model and the CGM sensor the patient uses, such as a MiniMed 780G pump with a Simplera Sync, Guardian 4, or Instinct sensor, and any separate transmitter.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Download library (US) ↗One page per product, with the current user guides. Old medtronicdiabetes.com links redirect here.minimed.comInstinct sensor support (US) ↗Support page and manual for the Instinct sensor, which is made by Abbott.minimed.com
  1. Open the US download library and choose the product page for the pump.
  2. Download the system user guide for that pump and sensor combination. Read "Exposure to magnetic fields and radiation" in its safety chapter.
  3. Download the sensor's own guide as well. The 780G guide states, "For the most current warnings, see the user guide that came with the device."
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The back cover shows the document number, revision, and date, for example "© 2026 Medtronic M072481C001_3 2026-02-15 MMT-1884, MMT-1894" for the US 780G guide.
  • US editions list US model numbers, such as MMT-1884 and MMT-1894 for the 780G, show the "Requires prescription in the USA" symbol, and use mg/dL. They do not carry a "Federal law" sentence.
  • The PDFs still print Medtronic and the Northridge, California address after the company split. That is expected for US editions.

! Watch For

  • Sensors used with the same pump have different labeling. Simplera, Simplera Sync, and Guardian 4 carry the MR Unsafe symbol. The Instinct manual says, "You can safely have a 1.5T or 3T MRI exam while wearing your sensor, under the conditions provided on the insert that came with your sensor." Check each component's own guide.
  • International 780G guides have the same title but different model numbers (MMT-1885 and MMT-1895), mmol/L, and a CE 0459 mark. Use the US edition.
  • Older US editions remain online at direct links. One 780G guide on an old medtronicdiabetes.com address is M072481C001_2, dated 2025-08-29, which _3 replaced. Start from the download library.
