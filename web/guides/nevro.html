Nevro Senza MRI Guidelines

Nevro publishes separate US MRI guidelines for different generator families. Its Product Manuals page lists the current edition for each region.

  • Category Neuromodulation
  • Covers Senza, Senza II, Senza Omnia, and Senza HFX iQ spinal cord stimulation systems
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the generator (IPG) model and every lead, extension, adaptor, and anchor model from the patient ID card or implant records.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Product Manuals for healthcare professionals ↗Choose the United States, "MRI Guidelines", and English. The list loads with JavaScript. The older nevro.com/manuals address redirects here.nevro.com
  1. Open Product Manuals and select United States, MRI Guidelines, and English.
  2. Pick the document for the generator family. On 2026-10-01 the US list showed one guideline for Senza HFX iQ (NIPG3000) and another for Senza, Senza II, and Senza Omnia (NIPG1000, NIPG1500, NIPG2000, NIPG2500).
  3. Note the revision shown in the list, then confirm the PDF footer shows the same revision.
  4. Check the component tables to make sure every implanted lead and accessory is listed for that generator's document.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The cover says "For United States of America only" and gives an effective date, for example "Effective July 2024".
  • The footer shows the document number and revision, for example "10001162 Rev D".
  • A CE mark statement inside a US document does not make it a European edition. Rely on the "United States of America" line and the US document number.

! Watch For

  • The Senza HFX name spans several product generations, but the US guidelines are split by generator model. Choose the document by IPG model, not by brand name.
  • The same page also returns European and Australian editions with similar titles.
  • Superseded US editions still load from old web addresses and appear in search results. Use the revision listed on the Product Manuals page.
  • Nevro states: "Use only product literature from the region where the patient procedure was performed."
Step 4 of 4

Check Notices and Related Pages

FDA Medical Device Recalls and Early Alerts ↗Nevro does not post a separate safety-notice page. Check FDA's list.fda.gov
Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Nevro publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
