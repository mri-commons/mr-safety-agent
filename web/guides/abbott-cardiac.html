Abbott Cardiac Device MRI Manuals

Abbott publishes MRI-Ready Systems manuals for its cardiac devices in its electronic instructions-for-use library. Different device families use different manuals, some with the same title.

  • Category Cardiac Devices
  • Covers Abbott MR Conditional pacemakers, ICDs, CRT devices, AVEIR leadless pacemakers, and leads. Abbott neuromodulation systems have a separate guide.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the exact generator model and every lead model and length, including capped or abandoned leads, from the device interrogation or patient ID card.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

It needs JavaScript.mri.merlin.netAbbott eIFU manual library ↗Choose the United States, English, and Health Care Professional, then search for "MRI-Ready" or the device name.manuals.eifu.abbott
  1. Use the MRI Ready product search to check the device and lead combination.
  2. In the eIFU library, select the United States and search for "MRI-Ready" with the device name, such as Assurity MRI, Gallant, or AVEIR.
  3. Open the manual whose combination tables name your device family, and confirm every lead model and length is listed for the field strength you need.
  4. Download a fresh copy for each scan.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The cover carries the US caution, for example "CAUTION: Federal (USA) law restricts this device to sale by or on the order of a physician."
  • The back page shows the date and document number, for example "2026-01" and "ARTEN600381891 A".
  • US editions sit under a /global/US/en/ path in the library. Other country codes are other markets' editions.

! Watch For

  • Two current US manuals share the title "MRI-Ready Systems Manual". On 2026-10-01, ARTEN600381891 A covered pacemakers such as Assurity MRI and Endurity MRI, and ARTEN600356465 A covered ICDs and CRT-Ds such as Avant and Gallant. Check the device family in the tables, not just the title.
  • AVEIR leadless pacemakers and insertable cardiac monitors have their own MRI manuals.
  • English-language editions for European countries sit beside the US ones, and retired US editions remain in the library. Being in English does not make a manual the US edition.
  • Abbott states: "If a device is not shown in the list, it is not MR Conditional."

Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Abbott publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
