Mentor Tissue Expander Instructions for Use

Johnson & Johnson MedTech publishes the US instructions for Mentor breast tissue expanders on its eIFU portal. The injection dome contains a magnet, and the labeling says MRI is not to be used.

  • Category Breast Tissue Expanders
  • Covers MENTOR CPX4, CPX4 with Suture Tabs, CPX4 PLUS Enhance, and ARTOURA breast tissue expanders (Johnson & Johnson MedTech).
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Find out which Mentor expander the patient has and when it was placed, from the operative note or implant card.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Johnson & Johnson eIFU portal ↗Choose healthcare professional and the United States, then search for the product name. Each entry shows its document number, version, and date.e-ifu.com
  1. Open the eIFU portal, choose the United States, and search for the product name, such as CPX4 or ARTOURA.
  2. Choose the entry whose reference numbers match the US models, and read the warning on magnetic fields.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • Each US instruction carries a LAB document number and version, for example "LAB100835493v3" for CPX4.
  • US editions state "Caution: Federal (U.S.A.) law restricts this device to sale by or on the order of a licensed healthcare practitioner" and list the Irving, Texas address with no European representative.
  • The symbol glossary includes MR Unsafe.

! Watch For

  • Mentor states: "MRI is not to be used on a patient implanted with these devices because movement could occur causing patient pain or expander displacement that could require revision surgery."
  • A CE-marked multilingual CPX4 edition, LAB100745420, appears in the same US search results. It uses different reference numbers and lists a European representative.
  • Older 2021 versions, LAB100835493v1 and LAB100835497v1, are still listed in US results with the same product codes. Choose the highest version, v3 as of October 2026.
  • The "MENTOR Smooth Tissue Expanders" with a remote dome are a different product for scar revision and tissue defects. They have MR Conditional labeling. Do not confuse them with the breast expanders.
  • The old mentorwwllc.com address redirects away from the instructions. Use the eIFU portal.
