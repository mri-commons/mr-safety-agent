LivaNova VNS Therapy MRI Guidance

LivaNova publishes its US MRI guidance for the VNS Therapy System as a physician's manual in an e-labeling portal. The PDFs and decision tool on its website are summaries.

  • Category Neuromodulation
  • Covers VNS Therapy generators such as SenTiva, AspireSR, AspireHC, DemiPulse, and Pulse, with Model 300-series leads
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the generator model and the lead model from the patient's records or ID card, and note where the generator is implanted.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

LivaNova e-labeling portal ↗Choose English-US. The page should say it shows manuals for the United States only. The MRI manual is under the generator and lead components. It needs JavaScript.dynamic.livanova.comMRI Safety for healthcare professionals (US) ↗Patient MRI form, a summary MRI guideline, and a decision tool. It points to the full MRI instructions for special cases.livanova.com
  1. Open the e-labeling portal, choose English-US, and confirm the page says it shows United States manuals.
  2. Download the MRI manual marked "(US)". The portal creates the file on request, so download a fresh copy each time.
  3. Confirm the generator and lead models are listed in the manual's applicability section, then follow the manual for that setup.
  4. Check the field safety notices listed in the same English-US portal view.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The edition label on the cover and in each page footer should end in "(US)", together with a month and year.
  • The portal offers no version history or fixed link, so the identity line printed on the PDF is the way to tell editions apart.

! Watch For

  • Other regions' editions of the same manual share its title. An English-EU view serves a "(CE)" edition. Make sure the label says "(US)".
  • LivaNova's MRI page says that special cases such as "potential lead breaks, partially explanted systems, remaining lead segments" need the MRI Instructions for Use, not the summary or decision tool.
  • Older summary PDFs remain online and appear in search results. Start from the portal.

Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where LivaNova publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
