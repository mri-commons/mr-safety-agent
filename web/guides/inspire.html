Inspire MRI Guidelines

Inspire publishes one MRI guidelines manual for its current systems on its e-labeling portal. The manual sets out numbered scan conditions, and which ones apply depends on the generator, the body region, and the coil.

  • Category Neuromodulation
  • Covers Inspire IV (generator 3028, with a sensing lead) and Inspire V (generator 3150, without a sensing lead) systems for obstructive sleep apnea, and the older Inspire II generator 3024.
Step 1 of 4

Gather Model Numbers

Get the generator model and serial number from the patient ID card, the implant record, or an X-ray, and every lead, including any abandoned lead or lead fragment from an earlier system.

Step 2 of 4

Find the Document

Inspire e-labeling portal ↗Choose "English - US" and open the MRI guidelines. Older revisions are under the archived manuals.manuals.inspiresleep.com
  1. Open the e-labeling portal with "English - US" selected and download the current MRI guidelines.
  2. Use the manual's tables in order. Check generator eligibility, the implant location, the scan condition for the body region and coil, and any abandoned leads.
  3. Have the patient confirm a working system with the sleep remote and turn therapy off before the scan, as the manual describes.
Step 3 of 4

Confirm It

✓ Check the Document

  • The back cover shows the document number, revision, and date, for example "200-556-101 REV E 2026 01" for the US guidelines.
  • US documents end in -101 and list only the Golden Valley, Minnesota address. Editions ending in -008 or -010 are for the UK and Europe.
  • Inspire states: "Before conducting an MRI scan, always ensure that you are using the most recent version of these MRI procedures."

! Watch For

  • Older US revisions allow 1.5T only. 3T was added in Rev E (2026), and only for some scan conditions. Copies of Rev B and earlier still rank high in search results. Download the guidelines from the portal.
  • The UK edition, "200-239-008 REV F 2022-10", covers only the 3028 generator and lists "MR Conditional at 1.5T". Use the US edition.
  • The Inspire II generator 3024 is "Not eligible for MRI." The manual also states: "An abandoned Inspire generator is MR Unsafe."
  • Inspire V has no sensing lead, but a patient upgraded from Inspire IV may still have an abandoned sensing lead. The manual's lead tables decide which scans remain possible.
  • The manual states: "Do not perform an MRI scan on patients who cannot confirm a functioning system."
Step 4 of 4

Check Notices and Related Pages

FDA recall database (product code MNQ) ↗Inspire has no US notice page. Search FDA records by product name or the code MNQ.accessdata.fda.govFDA Medical Device Recalls and Early Alerts ↗FDA's list of recalls and early alerts.fda.gov
Where to Look, Not Whether to Scan. This guide explains where Inspire publishes MRI documents and how to confirm you have the right one. It does not state scan conditions or decide whether a patient can be scanned. Read the applicable document in full and follow your MR safety team's procedures. Manufacturer sites and documents change, so always confirm the edition on the document itself.
