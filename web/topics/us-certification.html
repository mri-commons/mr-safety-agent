US MR Safety Certification: MRMD, MRSO, MRSE, and MRST

The International Board of Magnetic Resonance Safety (IBMRS) offers four MR safety credentials. Here is what each role does and how certification works, summarized from IBMRS's own pages.

  • Topic Certification

Four Credentials, Four Roles

IBMRS expects most MRSOs to be MR technologists and most MRSEs to be MR physicists, but this is not a requirement.

CredentialRole as IBMRS describes it
MRMDMR Medical Director or MR Physician. Responsible for establishing the site's MR safety policies and overseeing safety decisions.
MRSOMR Safety Officer. Carries out the site's MR safety practices as set by the MRMD, and advises the MRMD as needed.
MRSEMR Safety Expert. An advisory resource for the MRMD and MRSO, with greater depth in MR physics and safety.
MRSTMR Safety Tech. Assists the scanning technologist, including during remote scanning. At sites without a full technologist on site, oversees access, emergency response, and cryogens.

Sources: 1, 2

A Course Certificate Is Not a Credential

An IBMRS credential is earned by passing the IBMRS examination. A course completion certificate documents a course you took. IBMRS "does not require participation in any test preparation course" and "has not developed, nor endorsed" any training or exam-preparation courses.

Tip A credential is also separate from your job. Under the ACR manual, the facility's MR Medical Director appoints the MRSO and defines the training for Level 1 and Level 2 MR personnel.

Sources: 2, 5

The Exam

  • Only the MRMD exam has a prerequisite, a physician license. Anyone may take the MRSO, MRSE, or MRST exam.
  • 100 multiple-choice, true-false, and multiple-answer questions
  • Seven domains: static magnetic fields, time-varying gradient fields, radiofrequency fields, gadolinium and pregnancy, facilities and cryogens, standards and regulations, and clinical applications
  • US and international versions of each exam. Choose the one for where you live or work.
  • Taken at Prometric testing centers in North America, with government-issued photo ID that includes a signature

Sources: 2, 3

Attempts, Fees, and Renewal

  • US exam fee: $350 for each credential, as listed on 2026-10-01
  • Three attempts per credential, at least 60 days apart
  • Valid for 10 years from the date you pass. Renewal is by re-examination.
  • Continuing education is not required to renew.

Tip Fees and policies change. Check the registration page before you apply.

Sources: 3, 2

Plan Your Study

Start from the exam content syllabus for the credential you want, then compare its topics with your experience. IBMRS lists learning resources it recommends in its FAQ, while noting that it cannot endorse specific study materials. MR Safety Agent can turn the current syllabus into a suggested study plan.

Sources: 4, 2
